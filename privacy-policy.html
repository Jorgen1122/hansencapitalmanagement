Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Hansen Capital Management LLC ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through hansencapitalmanagement.com and our related services, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to visitors, bookkeeping and tax clients, and users of our client portal.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories, depending on how you interact with us:

CategoryExamples
Contact informationName, email address, phone number
Account informationLogin credentials, business name, account activity
Financial & tax informationBank and transaction records, financial statements, tax return data, capital gains/investment records, documents you upload to your client portal
Payment informationBilling details processed through Stripe (see Section 4) — we do not store your full card number
Scheduling informationInformation provided when booking a consultation through our scheduling tool
Technical informationIP address, browser type, general usage data collected automatically when you visit the site

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide bookkeeping, tax preparation, and advisory services you've engaged us for
  • To process payments for our services
  • To communicate with you about your account, engagement, or scheduled consultations
  • To maintain and secure your client portal account
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including tax and recordkeeping requirements
  • To improve our website and services
Tax return information is handled separately. If you're a tax preparation client, your tax return information is also governed by IRC Section 7216 and its regulations. We will not disclose or use your tax return information for purposes other than preparing your return without your separate, signed written consent, as required by law.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our business (see Section 4), under obligations to protect your data
  • Government or regulatory authorities, where required by law
  • Professional advisors (e.g., a CPA or attorney you've asked us to coordinate with), with your direction

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following third-party services to operate our business. Each has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on our behalf:

ProviderPurpose
StripePayment processing. Stripe collects and processes your payment card information directly; we do not store your full card number. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.
SupabaseClient portal authentication, database, and secure document storage
CalendlyConsultation scheduling. See Calendly's Privacy Policy.

Google AnalyticsAggregate website traffic measurement on our public pages. Not used on client portal pages. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission, access controls restricting your data to your own account, and file-type and size validation on document uploads. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain client financial and tax information for as long as needed to provide our services and to comply with applicable recordkeeping and tax laws, which may require retention beyond the end of our engagement.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, or to opt out of certain processing. To make a request, contact us using the information in Section 10. We will respond consistent with applicable law.

8. Cookies & Tracking

Our public web pages use Google Analytics only if you choose "Accept" in the cookie banner. Until you accept, Google Analytics is not loaded and sets no cookies; if you decline, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, it stays off. Your choice is saved in your browser, and you can change it at any time with the "Cookie Settings" link at the bottom of every page. When enabled, Google Analytics sets cookies to measure aggregate traffic, such as pages visited, referring sites, general location, and whether a visitor booked a consultation or clicked to call or email us. We do not use these cookies for advertising, and Google Analytics does not run on client portal pages (login, dashboard, and password reset), so no portal or financial data is sent to Google. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout or by blocking cookies in your browser. Our embedded scheduling tool (Calendly) may set its own cookies to operate the booking widget, governed by its own privacy policy linked above.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are directed at businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

Bookkeeping, tax, and analytics services are offered separately from investment management activities and do not constitute investment advice.
