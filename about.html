About Hansen Capital Management

Hansen Capital Management LLC is a fund management firm focused on quantitative trading strategies, systematic research, and disciplined risk management.

About the Firm

Hansen Capital Management LLC develops and manages proprietary trading processes designed to support the investment objectives of the Hansen Capital Fund.

The firm's investment philosophy emphasizes systematic research, statistical analysis, disciplined execution, and risk oversight. Hansen Capital Management seeks to maintain a structured approach to market research and portfolio management through internally developed methodologies.

The firm does not publicly disclose proprietary signal logic, model architecture, or trading methodology.

Firm Structure

Hansen Capital Management LLC serves as the General Partner of the Hansen Capital Fund and is responsible for market research, portfolio management, risk oversight, and the implementation of proprietary trading processes designed to support the fund's investment objectives.

Hansen Capital Management LLC operates as a subsidiary of Hansen Holdings LLC, which serves as the parent company of the Hansen organization.

Founder

Jorgen Hansen | Founder & Managing Member

Jorgen Hansen is the Founder and Managing Member of Hansen Capital Management LLC.

His background includes experience in financial services, securities markets, trading operations, and investment research. Through professional experience and independent quantitative research, he developed a strong interest in systematic trading, portfolio management, and risk-controlled investment processes.

Hansen founded the firm with the objective of building a research-driven fund management organization focused on quantitative trading methodologies, disciplined execution, and long-term capital stewardship.

Mission

Hansen Capital Management was founded on the belief that effective fund management requires discipline, research, and risk awareness. The firm's mission is to develop and manage quantitative trading processes designed to support the Hansen Capital Fund's investment objectives while maintaining a strong commitment to risk oversight and continuous improvement.
